1. About KakeStats

KakeStats is an independent Discord application for the Kakele MMORPG community. It provides game rankings, character statistics, game wiki lookups, configurable notifications and information about active game events. KakeStats is not affiliated with, endorsed by or operated by the creators of Kakele or Discord. These terms govern your use of KakeStats, not the underlying game or Discord.

2. Eligibility and access

You must meet Discord’s minimum age and account requirements and any higher minimum age required by applicable law. Use of KakeStats must comply with Discord’s Terms of Service, its Community Guidelines and applicable game rules. Server administrators must have authority to install and configure the application for their server.

3. Using the service

The application currently provides ranking and experience-gain commands, monster, item and NPC lookups, boost information, language preferences, a server settings panel and automated boost/event notifications. Administrative tools are restricted to authorized users or the application operator. Server administrators are responsible for choosing appropriate channels and notification settings, including mentions.

KakeStats is currently offered without a separate usage fee. Any future paid features would be subject to separate disclosed terms and applicable consumer protections.

4. Acceptable use

Do not use KakeStats to harass, spam, impersonate, threaten or deceive others; bypass access restrictions or rate limits; overload the service; exploit security vulnerabilities; collect or expose other users’ personal information unlawfully; or engage in unlawful activity. Report security issues privately to the operator rather than exploiting them.

5. Game data and accuracy

Game information is obtained from Kakele’s public ranking files, game information endpoints and wiki. Ranking files are periodically updated and may not reflect live gameplay. Notifications, event countdowns and wiki information can be delayed, incomplete or incorrect. Use the game’s official information for important decisions. Rights in game names, artwork and other third-party materials remain with their respective owners.

6. Availability and changes

The service depends on Discord, Kakele and hosting providers. Maintenance, outages, changes to third-party services or technical limits may interrupt it. Features may be changed, suspended or discontinued for operational, security or legal reasons. Access may be restricted in response to abuse or violations, with notice where appropriate and practicable.

7. Privacy and support

Our Privacy Policy describes the information processed by KakeStats, including operational logs, preferences and direct-message forwarding to the operator. Do not send passwords, tokens, payment information or other sensitive information to the bot. For questions, abuse reports or privacy requests, use the contact details below.

Changes will be published on this page with an updated effective date. Material changes will be communicated where required by applicable law.

3. Purposes and legal bases

Where the GDPR applies, the intended legal bases are:

  • Service delivery (Article 6(1)(b), where applicable): responding to commands, saving chosen preferences and providing requested server notifications.
  • Legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f), subject to necessity and balancing): operating and securing the service, diagnosing errors, preventing abuse, handling support messages and maintaining necessary operational records.
  • Legal obligations (Article 6(1)(c)): responding to binding lawful requests and meeting applicable legal duties.

Operational records currently include identifiable activity and can be viewed through restricted reports. Such records must be limited to what is necessary for service operation; service-improvement analytics should use aggregated or de-identified information. KakeStats does not request payment details for its current features.

Providing command inputs is necessary for the requested command to work; storing a personal language is optional. You can choose not to send support messages. You can stop using the bot or remove it from a server if you have the required permission.

4. Direct messages and operator access

Important: Direct messages to KakeStats are not confidential conversations with an automated system. The current bot forwards ordinary incoming DMs to the operator, including message content and attachment links.

The operator can access restricted activity reports and server-specific bot settings, send messages using the bot and delete messages where Discord permissions permit. Forwarded DM copies are separate Discord messages in the operator’s inbox, not entries in the 30-day activity log. Do not use the bot to share secrets or sensitive personal data. For a private data request, use the email contact below.

5. Recipients and external services

  • Discord: interactions, messages, attachments and notifications are processed through Discord’s platform.
  • FPS.ms: hosts the bot and its local database and log files; see its Privacy Policy.
  • Kakele: the bot fetches public game data and images from Kakele. These requests use game-related paths and do not intentionally transmit Discord user IDs or ordinary DM text.
  • Operator: can access necessary configuration, diagnostics, log exports and forwarded DMs.
  • GitHub Pages: if this site is hosted there, GitHub handles website requests, including security logging of visitor IP addresses; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Information may also be disclosed when legally required. The service is not intended to sell personal data, share it for targeted advertising or profile users for unrelated purposes.

7. Your rights and requests

Depending on applicable law, you may request access to your personal data, correction, deletion, restriction, objection or portability. Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it without affecting prior lawful processing. You may lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority; in Estonia, contact the Data Protection Inspectorate.

Send a request to the contact below with a short description and your Discord user ID or relevant server ID. Do not provide passwords or tokens. The operator may request proportionate identity verification and will respond promptly, within applicable legal deadlines (normally one month under the GDPR). Requests concerning Discord’s own systems should be directed to Discord.

8. Security

The bot restricts its administrative commands and log exports to the configured owner and server settings to authorized administrators. Log text is limited and obvious credentials are redacted where detected. No system is entirely secure. If information has been collected from an ineligible child, contact the operator so that appropriate action can be taken.
